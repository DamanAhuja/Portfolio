Smart
Buggy

GGMR Group, 2026
Android & dispatch

Apps for the buggy service that carries passengers with reduced mobility at Terminal 3, IGI Airport.

Smart Buggy is four apps: a passenger kiosk, a driver tablet, a dispatch engine, and an admin portal. I build the kiosk and the driver tablet in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

The tablet publishes indoor position, battery, and seats over MQTT every second. A buggy with a stale heartbeat, a low battery, or a charging state drops out of the pool. Riders headed to the same pier share a vehicle, up to five seats, before an idle buggy is called. The sketch above shows those rules, not a live feed.

I also built an ARCore proof of concept that checked the airport’s vendor-led AR wayfinding flow before rollout.

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Node.js · MQTT · PostgreSQL · ARCore · Situm
